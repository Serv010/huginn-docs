# Roadmap

## Strategic Direction

Huginn roadmap focuses on expanding from a high-quality Solana intelligence core into cross-chain institutional wallet intelligence infrastructure.

## Phase 1: Solana Intelligence Core (Completed)

### Outcomes

- production wallet tracking workflows
- realized PnL and trade-quality dashboard metrics
- 30-day backfill with deep-scan recovery path
- Token-2022-aware parsing and metadata enrichment

## Phase 2: Institutional Workflow Hardening (2026 H1)

### Milestones

- role-based watchlist collaboration for teams
- expanded alert routing (webhooks and structured event feeds)
- richer risk diagnostics for concentration and liquidity
- stronger observability and operational audit trails

### Success Criteria

- lower unknown-basis trade ratio on newly added wallets
- reduced time-to-first-actionable-analytics after onboarding
- improved alert precision under high-throughput conditions

## Phase 3: Multi-Chain Intelligence Expansion (2026 H2)

### Milestones

- full EVM network analytics parity with Solana metric surface
- cross-chain wallet identity clustering and behavior linking
- per-network reliability scoring for ingestion confidence

### Success Criteria

- consistent analytics schema across Solana and EVM
- stable multi-network ingestion under peak volatility windows

## Phase 4: Advanced Intelligence Layer (2027)

### Milestones

- wallet cohort models by strategy archetype
- risk-adjusted wallet ranking enhancements
- institutional API SLAs and premium data delivery tiers

### Success Criteria

- stronger predictive persistence across strategy cohorts
- broader enterprise integration footprint

## Product Principles Across All Phases

- read-only intelligence first
- deterministic metric definitions
- explicit confidence and risk framing
- operational resilience before feature breadth
